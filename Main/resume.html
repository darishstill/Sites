Practice values

The NASW Code of Ethics, in practice.

Practicum interest: Macro-level practice serving men navigating faith, trauma, substance use, and health inequity: educating communities and informing stakeholders and policy officials.

Service

I stabilize before I accelerate: aftercare plans across eight goal areas, and MAT care kept unbroken from residential to community services.

Social justice

Systems fail people by responding to fragments. I secure housing and benefits continuity so discharge doesn't become one more.

Dignity & worth of the person

A person's worth doesn't depend on identity, history, or circumstances. I practice trauma-informed, strengths-based case management.

Importance of human relationships

Safety, then trust, then disclosure, then root-cause work. I let a guarded person set the pace, using Motivational Interviewing.

Integrity

I keep clear boundaries between case management and clinical or prescriber roles, and document to DDAP and Title 28 standards.

Competence

I hold the PA CRS credential, supervise and train interns, and am completing my BASW on the path to the MSW and LCSW.
